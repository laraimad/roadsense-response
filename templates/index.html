From detection to action

One focused workflow for safer roads.

Raw detections are not useful until a person can understand the evidence, make a decision, and hand work to a repair team.

01

Detect

A vehicle captures camera, accelerometer, GPS, and model signals.

02

Prioritize

Evidence is combined into an explainable severity score and repeat detections are grouped.

03

Respond

An operator verifies the incident, assigns a team, tracks status, and exports the repair queue.

Challenge prototype

Built as an isolated product layer

This app uses copied, anonymized demo records. It does not import, modify, or depend on the original FYP source, models, configuration, labels, or live data.

Responsible use

A decision aid, not an engineering certification.

The priority score is experimental. A trained person must verify the road condition before repair, closure, or safety decisions.
