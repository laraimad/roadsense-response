# Shortcut Asia Internship Challenge 2026 Requirements

Source reviewed: `[MMU] Shortcut Asia Internship Challenge 2026.pdf`, 9 pages.

## Deadline and process

- Build period stated in the brief: June 9-15, 2026.
- Submission deadline: **June 15, 2026 at 11:59 PM**.
- Submission form: <https://forms.gle/bTCqepJRpJYqYvkH6>
- Shortlisted applicants chat with a developer and later pitch at the Bangsar office. The pitch date is not yet confirmed in the brief.

## Required submission

1. A functional prototype, preferably deployed, or clear setup instructions.
2. A GitHub, GitLab, or Bitbucket repository. It may be public or shared privately with Shortcut Asia.
3. Brief documentation, recommended at 1-2 pages, covering:
   - Planning and approach.
   - Tools, frameworks, and libraries chosen.
   - Main technical decisions and reasoning.
   - One or two feature flowcharts.
   - A simple technical architecture overview.
   - Tech stack.
4. A demo video showing the product, key functionality, process, and decisions.
   - Three to five minutes is recommended but is not a strict limit.
   - A face recording is optional; voice-over is recommended.
   - Host it as an unlisted YouTube video or on Google Drive and submit the link.

## Evaluation

- Code: structure, clarity, logic, and implementation quality.
- Product: working functionality and how effectively it solves the problem.
- Communication: demo and supporting documentation.
- UX and usability are evaluated alongside technical strength.
- The brief recommends two solid, well-developed features over many incomplete features.

## Use of an existing FYP

The FAQ explicitly allows an existing passion project. It also states that the participant keeps full ownership of everything built and may continue using it as a portfolio project.

This does **not** replace MMU or supervisor rules. Before publishing anything from the original FYP, confirm university intellectual-property, confidentiality, research-data, and assessment rules. This challenge version avoids that risk by being a separate, self-contained product layer with copied demo assets only.

## What the PDF does not contain

The supplied PDF is a challenge brief and FAQ, not a complete legal agreement. It does not state detailed privacy, liability, university-IP, dataset licensing, or confidentiality clauses. Check the submission form for any additional consent text before pressing submit.
